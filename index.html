About the Project

The Women Safety Aware Navigation System is a smart web application designed to enhance women's safety during travel. It aims to provide secure route awareness, emergency assistance, and location-based safety support through an easy-to-use interface.

Our Features

🟢 Safe Route

Suggests safer travel routes for women.

📍 Live Location

Helps users share their current location during travel.

🆘 SOS Alert

Allows users to trigger an emergency alert quickly.

📞 Emergency Contacts

Stores important contacts for emergencies.

How It Works

1️⃣ Open Website

Launch the Women Safety Aware Navigation System.

2️⃣ Allow Location

Allow location access to detect your current position.

3️⃣ Choose Destination

Enter your destination to begin navigation.

4️⃣ Find Safe Route

The system identifies safer travel routes.

5️⃣ Travel Safely

Reach your destination and use SOS if required.

Emergency SOS

In case of an emergency, press the SOS button below to activate the emergency alert.
